01Positioning & principles

Institutional quant identities (Two Sigma, Citadel Securities, Jane Street, D.E. Shaw, Hudson River Trading, Jump, DRW, Millennium) are wordmark-led and typographically restrained. Credibility in this sector reads as precision and restraint, not iconographic novelty. The mandate rules out generic AI logos, ordinary cryptocurrency branding and meaningless futuristic decoration, so every element here carries a stated reason:

Monochrome first

Every mark is designed and tested in one colour before accent is allowed. If a mark only works in colour, it does not work.

Geometry over decoration

All three directions are built from one shared motif — a diamond node on a 96-unit grid — and nothing else. No gradients, no glows, no ornament.

Tiered responsive mark

A detailed lockup for large formats, a solid mark for small ones, measured at 16–96 px rather than assumed (§7).

Family motif: the node. A diamond placed where a decision is gated — the point at which independent signals converge and are verified before anything passes. The same motif recurs in all three directions and in the wordmark's hyphen position.

02Colour system — evidence, not taste

The product already owns five semantic hues (risk red 5.6°, amber 36.8°, gain green 145.4°, info cyan 204.1°, model purple 282.6° — docs/03 §3.1). A brand accent must sit ≥30° away from all five in hue, or dashboards will read brand chrome as system state. A sweep of the hue circle leaves exactly three free windows: 67–115°, 235–252°, 313–335°. 04Wordmark & lockups

The wordmark is drawn, not typed: eight monoline glyphs on the same 8-unit discipline as the mark (cap 48, stroke 7, tracking 10), so mark and word share one stroke language and the identity never depends on a licensed face. 07Misuse — forbidden treatments

stretch or squash

glows, shadows, bevels

gradients on the mark

off-system recolouring

rotation

outline / hollow forms

busy or photographic ground

recolouring the node to a semantic hue

The last tile is the dangerous one: a green node reads as profit, a red node as loss. The node is brand violet or the field colour — never a state colour.

08Logo animation concept — “the gate opens”

One motion idea for all directions: the node appears first, then the structure reveals out from it — a decision verified, then executed. For Direction B, expressed as mask reveals over the finished mark (so motion never redraws geometry):

0–200 ms · node scales in, ease-out

200–450 ms · stems grow out of the node

450–700 ms · diagonal draws toward the gate

700 ms · stops one ring short of the node

700–1200 ms · wordmark tracks in, opacity 0→1

Spec: 1.2 s total, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1), no loops, no glow, no colour change. prefers-reduced-motion renders the final frame statically. The same beat sheet maps onto A (rays retract from the node) and C (blades close onto it) if either is chosen. Motion stays a concept at Stage 1; it becomes buildable assets in Stage 2.
